Arborist Practice Questions is a set of free, interactive study activities built to support the PNW-ISA Certified Arborist Prep Course.

Objectives & Background

Preparing for the ISA Certified Arborist exam means working through a wide body of knowledge, from tree biology and soil science to diagnosis, field operations, and the management of whole urban forests. Reading alone rarely makes that knowledge stick. This website gives course participants a place to practice: each lesson generates new questions every time, checks answers immediately, and explains where an answer went wrong.

The activities are organized into five domains, each covering a part of the course content.
