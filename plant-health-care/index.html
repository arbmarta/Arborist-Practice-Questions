Disorders & Disturbances

Plant Health Care is broader than treating pests. It aims to maintain plant health, prevent problems, monitor change, and use appropriate controls only when needed.

Tree health and defense

The course describes a healthy tree as healthy in appearance, structure, and vitality. Vitality is the plant's ability to overcome stress and thrive, while vigor is its genetic capacity to resist stress. Arborists are usually concerned with vitality.

Plant defenses include physical structures and chemical compounds. Management can support defense by matching species to sites, selecting resistant species or cultivars, maintaining landscape diversity, and choosing appropriate timing for practices such as pruning.

Monitoring

Regular inspections collect information about the site, plant, and disorders and can quantify pest populations or disorder severity. Action thresholds can vary with plant health, phenology, and timing. The course distinguishes key stressors—frequently encountered pests, disorders, or site conditions—and key plants, which may be species with frequent pest problems or plants of high client value.

Management goals

Prevention

The preferred approach: promote plant health and prevent problems before they begin.

Suppression

Reduce a pest population to a tolerable level.

Eradication

Total removal of a pest from an area.

Control strategies

PHC and IPM can combine cultural, mechanical, biological, and chemical controls. Biological control may involve introduction, conservation, or augmentation of predators, parasites, or pathogens. Chemical controls require attention to nontarget effects, pest resurgence, and pesticide resistance. Alternative pesticide options in the course include insecticidal soaps, horticultural oils, botanical pesticides, insect growth regulators, and microbial pesticides.

Check: Why isn't “eradicate every pest” the default PHC objective?

PHC focuses on maintaining plant health and managing pests to tolerable levels. IPM decisions use monitoring and thresholds and seek effective control with minimal harmful effects and disturbance.
